## 3. Provenance and limitations

The product size and black finish come from [Accu](https://www.accu.co.uk/pozi-countersunk-screws/988564-SPK-M4-30-CS-BL); [Westfield Fasteners](https://www.westfieldfasteners.co.uk/Bolts-Screws-Metric/Machine-Screw-Phillips-Countersunk-M4x75-A2-Stainless.html) corroborates the DIN 965 M4 head dimensions.

The 24-sided head preserves the exact 7.5 mm X/Y envelope. The right-hand external thread is a continuous helix with a flat, lightly chamfered end; the Pozidriv recess is a cavity. Recess-wing detail and the 3.14 mm root diameter are representative where drawings are incomplete. The closed manifold has 1,562 vertices and 3,120 triangles.
